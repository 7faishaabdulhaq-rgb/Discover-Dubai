About Dubai

Dubai is a major global city and one of the seven emirates that make up the United Arab Emirates (UAE) on the Persian Gulf.

For a long time, Dubai was a small, quiet village. People survived by fishing and diving deep into the sea to find pearls.

History of Dubai

Dubai started as a small fishing and pearl-diving village in the 18th century and grew into a local trading hub near Dubai Creek.

The discovery of oil in 1966 brought wealth, funding new infrastructure and leading Dubai to join the United Arab Emirates in 1971.

Dubai shifted its focus to tourism, trade, and real estate, building world-famous landmarks like the Burj Khalifa to become a major global city.
